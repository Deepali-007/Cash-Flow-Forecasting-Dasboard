# Cash Flow Forecasting & Dashboard

## Project Overview
This project focuses on monthly cash flow forecasting using Microsoft Excel and Power BI. It analyzes expected cash inflows and outflows to understand projected cash positions and monitor potential cash surpluses or shortfalls.

The project demonstrates the use of financial calculations, forecasting techniques, and data visualization to present cash flow information in a structured and understandable format.

## Objectives
- Prepare monthly cash flow forecasts.
- Analyze projected cash inflows and outflows.
- Calculate forecast net cash flow.
- Monitor projected closing cash balances.
- Identify potential cash surpluses and shortfalls.
- Present forecast information through an interactive dashboard.

## Tools & Technologies
- **Microsoft Excel:** Cash flow calculations, forecast preparation, data organization, and financial analysis.
- **Microsoft Power BI:** Dashboard development, KPI visualization, and monthly cash flow trend analysis.

## Key Analysis Performed
- Organized monthly cash flow information for forecasting.
- Analyzed forecast cash inflows and cash outflows.
- Calculated monthly forecast net cash flow.
- Evaluated projected closing cash balances.
- Reviewed monthly cash surplus and shortfall trends.
- Used the opening cash balance as the starting point for the forecast.

## Dashboard Features
The Power BI dashboard includes:
- Forecast Cash Inflow vs. Outflow
- Forecast Closing Cash Balance Trend
- Monthly Cash Surplus / Shortfall Trend
- Monthly Forecast Net Cash Flow
- Key cash flow indicators and summary metrics

## Project Files
- `Cash_Flow_Forecasting.xlsx` – Excel workbook containing the cash flow forecast and supporting calculations.
- `PowerBI_Dashboard.pbix` – Power BI report containing the cash flow dashboard.

## Skills Demonstrated
Cash Flow Analysis | Financial Forecasting | Microsoft Excel | Power BI | Financial Calculations | KPI Analysis | Data Visualization | Financial Reporting

## Project Outcome
Developed a monthly cash flow forecasting model and an interactive dashboard to present projected inflows, outflows, net cash flow, and closing cash balances. The dashboard provides a clear view of expected cash positions and potential cash flow shortfalls.
